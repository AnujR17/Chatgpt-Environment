What changed from iteration 1

Removed owner decisions

  • Claim-window, reply-by and regulatory clocks from Insights (reply-by stays in the chat header)
  • "No review triggers", "Permission" and "History: C 3/7…" chips
  • P3 claim window and T3 late filing (the 24 h limit is now an upstream gate; S7 removed)
  • Restaurant permission block, request / remind / escalate, 10-minute wait
  • OTP and location ping from Evidence (proof of delivery moved to the Rider view)
  • People tab (names now sit in Party records)
  • Ask for evidence, Internal note and reference wording in the reply box
  • P2 default liability as a visible row (now only in the P1 ⓘ detail)

Added new

  • Centre switch: Customer chat · Rider · Restaurant, with unread badge and one-line preview
  • Rider view: activity log, proof of delivery, trip time vs promised, contacts, incidents, R1–R5
  • Restaurant view: acceptance and prep vs own estimate, KOT, instructions, substitutions, terms
  • Case insight line (Customer · Rider · Restaurant), clickable to sources
  • Terms block (restaurant terms + triggers only when fired)
  • Photo tags the agent taps to confirm; condition override with "Changed by agent"
  • On hold with "who is responsible", Held tag in the queue
  • Close-ticket pop-up and 3–4 closing messages; restaurant report on close
  • Suggested reply from keywords; templates only for unmet / unknown conditions

Changed changed

  • Subject pre-filled from the bot, 17 options; Fault and Action never pre-filled
  • Action has five buttons; Reason list depends on Fault × Action
  • Full refund = affected items + GST; whole order for non-delivery
  • P6 is now "within the restaurant's agreed terms"; cost split shows Swiggy's share above the cap
  • Party records on one basis: raised · upheld out of total, with a total row for the customer
  • Records side by side in every case; Similar cases became the on-demand Star case with a QA fairness rating
  • Evidence scrolls sideways; timeline shows the complaint gap ("+8 min")
  • Case log opens as a centred pop-up

Notes for review

Labelled assumptions

  • Rider-app data (location at marking, call logs, incidents, "marked packed") is Hypothetical in shape.
  • GST shown at 5%, the rate on restaurant food sold through a delivery platform.
  • Refund timelines in closing messages ("3–5 working days") and the 3–4 min ideal handling time are placeholders.
  • Suggested replies use simple keyword rules here; the real feature would generate text.

Risks carried to Stage 12

  • Legal deadline (48 h / 1 month) is no longer on screen, but held and review cases stay open.
  • Suggested replies are unrestricted and can promise an outcome before Confirm; only the log guards this.
  • The star case can still anchor agents; Stage 9 should check copy rates.
  • Non-delivery cases now rest on one system fact: rider location at marking.
