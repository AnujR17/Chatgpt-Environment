What changed in this version, and what the fit test found

Evidence: delivery photo removed

Riders and restaurants log no photos (owner decision, policy v1.3). The evidence block now holds the customer's photo, gap facts and system records (OTP, location ping) only. Case information now fits above the fold in S2, S3, S5 and S8.

Policy v1.3

Policy conditions: one line each

Each condition is a single row (status · name · fact). Source, evidence and the threshold note open on click. The block went from about 430 px to about 200 px in S2.

I2

Fit test: all four chats fit

Measured at 1440 × 900 with expandable rows closed: case information, chat and insights fit without scrolling in S2, S3, S5 and S8, including S3 with a decision open. When the check step is open, the conditions area shrinks to about 170 px; watch whether agents re-read conditions there (Stage 9). Fonts were a fallback in the test browser (±20 px).

Verified

Still open from v1 (owner decisions)

  • Permission is still shown in M4, the status-strip chip (I1.3) and P6.
  • History still repeats in the status strip (I1.4).
  • Partial-refund items appear only after pressing Partial (built that way; recode I4.2 from A to H if approved).

New fairness point (v1.3)

With no rider or restaurant photos, every photo comes from the customer, the party making the claim. The screen states the origin as a fact ("sent in afterwards") and never rates it. Logged for Stage 12.

Limitations

Assumptions in this frame

  • Assumption: P7 and P8 stay Unknown until the agent picks an amount, then update live.
  • Assumption: when the agent refunds a customer-fault case, cost falls on Swiggy as goodwill [Inferred]; Swiggy publishes no rule.
  • Assumption: a fault and a reason are required before confirming any outcome except Send to review.
  • Similar-case outcomes, names, orders, times and counts are fictional placeholders.
