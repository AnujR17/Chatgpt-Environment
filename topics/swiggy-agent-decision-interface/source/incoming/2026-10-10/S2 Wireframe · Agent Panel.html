What the fit test found

Measured in the 1440 × 900 frame with every G chip collapsed. Fonts were a fallback in the test browser, so treat pixel numbers as approximate (±20 px).

1 · Case info overflows by about 66 px

Restaurant permission (M4, all A) and People (M5) fall below the fold. The order, evidence and party records fit.

Cause: permission is shown three times: M4 block, status-strip chip (I1.3) and condition P6.

Decision needed

2 · History sits in the status strip

I1.4 repeats the three party counts next to the clocks and triggers, right above the decision buttons. That is the most decision-adjacent spot on screen for something labelled "Context, not a policy condition". Fairness risk: it reads like a fourth trigger.

Decision needed

3 · Partial refund items open on click

I4.2 is coded A, but item checkboxes for every case would push the decision bar up ~70 px. In the frame they appear only after "Partial" is pressed. Proposed recode: button A, checkboxes H.

Recode

4 · Insights column fits

Status strip, issue, P3–P8 and the pinned decision bar fit without scrolling in S2. Scenarios with triggers (S3, S4) add chips and the I6 side-by-side block, so that column needs its own fit test there.

OK in S2

5 · Queue rail codes

Two-letter issue codes (WI, MI, LD, DM) fit 56 px but must be learned. Test with the new-agent persona; the hover overlay gives full words.

Stage 9 watch

6 · Chat has spare height

Chat ends ~100 px above the reply box at this point of the conversation. It fills as the chat grows, so no change.

No change

Assumptions in this frame

  • Assumption: P7 and P8 show "Unknown" until the agent enters an amount, because both check the refund amount, which does not exist yet.
  • Assumption: P4 (cash on delivery) is hidden because S2 is prepaid. It is coded H.
  • Assumption: restaurant permission is already given in S2, so S2 tests evidence and history only. S5 covers the stalled case.
  • All names, order numbers, times and counts are fictional. Past-case outcomes in the similar-cases drawer are placeholders, shown without counts.
