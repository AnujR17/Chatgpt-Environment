# Expert Interview Guide — former Amex design-team lead

1. In your time leading design at Amex, did you ever work on a tool used by an internal reviewer — dispute, chargeback, fraud, claims — to decide a case? Or was your work mostly customer-facing?
2. When a tool surfaced a pattern or flag to a human reviewer, how did you decide what it could *show* versus what it could *decide*? Was there ever pressure to just have it tell the reviewer the answer?
3. When a case involved two conflicting sides — a cardholder and a merchant, say — how did the screen handle showing both without picking one?
4. What's one layout choice you made, or saw fail, to keep a dense case reviewable in seconds, not minutes?
5. How did the reviewer know *why* a flag appeared, rather than just trusting it?
