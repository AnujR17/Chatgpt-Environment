Support Agent Decision Interface — Research working note

Who checks what, before a refund is decided

Every parameter behind a Swiggy refund or account-block decision, split by where in the process it appears to enter: the Help-chat bot, the human agent, and the backend teams neither of them show in real time.

⚠ Assumption, labelled: Swiggy has never published which team sees which signal. The Bot / Agent / Backend split below is our own reconstruction, built from the process description in the research notes (Section 5) and one anonymised support-executive interview. Every row keeps its original evidence tag so you can tell a documented rule from a reported pattern from our inference.

Rows 10-12 are flagged, not proposed

  • Value tier and fraud flag are the two places the research drifts from a checkable fact into a customer-intent score. Surfacing either one to an agent means the interface is scoring the customer, not showing what they did — this breaks the project's no-prediction rule directly.
  • Device signals (SHIELD) are reported for promo-abuse and delivery-incentive fraud. Nothing in the sources ties them to refund decisions specifically, so including them here would be a guess dressed up as data.
  • Objective alternative already in the research notes: show refund count and complaint count as plain numbers with dates (row 8-9), and let the agent draw their own conclusion — never a pre-computed tier or flag.
