Interview guide

Play and goals

1. Tell me about your most recent gaming session. What did you want from it? 2. Describe a moment when that session became less enjoyable. 3. What made that moment matter to you? 4. Tell me about a difficult moment you still enjoyed. What was different?

Response and timing

5. What changed in how you played, moved or communicated? 6. How did you decide whether to continue or stop? 7. What did you do to make the situation better? 8. When would support have fitted into that session? When would it have interrupted you?

Concept and control

9. Looking at the companion concept, what would you use, change or ignore? 10. Which sources would you choose to enable: check-in, camera, game observation or input timing? 11. If the companion misunderstood the moment, how would you want to correct it? 12. What would make you keep this app, and what would make you remove it?
