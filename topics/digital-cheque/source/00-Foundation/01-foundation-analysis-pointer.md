# Digital Cheque — Foundation Analysis (pointer)

Living doc: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/4dc58b2b-e509-4434-9570-797f46f794ff (as of 2026-09-23)

Key decisions/findings so far (secondary research + hypotheses; no primary research yet):
- Core insight (hypothesis): a cheque's real product is legal leverage for the payee; payees demand it, payers tolerate it.
- Legal hook: NI Act s.6 already includes "cheque in the electronic form" (IT Act signatures); PSS Act 2007 s.25 mirrors s.138 for failed electronic funds transfers.
- Gap is composition: no P2P, future-dated, high-value, payee-holdable commitment on any digital rail.
- Proposed segment priority: 1) MSME supplier-buyer, 2) tenants/landlords (to confirm with user).
- Next stage: 12–16 interviews + 3 expert interviews (advocate, bank ops, CA).
