# Test facial movements with a local video

Enable **Track face and hands**, choose **Open video**, and select an MP4, MOV, AVI or MKV on your computer. Opening a video stops the webcam source and runs the same physical-face model on the clip. Nori updates in the main screen, the desktop overlay and the paired phone. The camera panel labels the source **Video file**. To repeat the test, reopen the clip; to return to your own face, stop the video and choose Start camera.

Use one clearly visible face with ordinary lighting. A sequence containing a relaxed face, smile, open smile/laughter-like movement and drawn brows lets you compare the character with the visible movement. Spoken/open-mouth movement alone should not turn into an open smile. Head turns, occlusion, small/multiple faces and lighting can legitimately make tracking unavailable; Nori should then look neutral and show tracking status.

A repeatable clip is useful for comparing versions, but testing someone else does not establish accuracy for your own face or camera. Use a fresh independently labelled clip if measuring a model; do not infer emotion ground truth from a title or an assumed expression. No audio is analysed, weights are not retrained and no personal emotion-photo set is needed. Your reported CMA remains separate. The included recorded-video software checks and synthetic animation previews are different evidence, not a combined accuracy claim.
